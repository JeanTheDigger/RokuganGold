Player Cheat Sheet

Type / in any channel, pick a command, then fill in its options. /help opens the full manual.

A | B means pick one. Grey words are options. You have one character, so you never need to name it.

Start here

/sheet create
Build your character in a private channel. Staff approve it.
/whoami
Your home screen, with buttons for Void, kata, gear and more.
/sheet view
Your full character sheet.
/inventory
Wield weapons, wear armor, drop things, check your purse.
/portrait set
Add a picture of your character.
/help
The full manual, by topic.

Roll dice

/roll rolled kept
Roll and Keep. Only you see it unless you change secret.
/dice 5k3
The quick way. Also 7k2+5.
/check skill | social | stealth | investigate | lore | craft
A skill roll against a TN your GM names. Every one but skill picks the trait for you.
/macro save | roll
Save a roll you repeat, then roll it by name.
/history
The latest rolls in this channel.
Add spend_void to a check for +1k1. Use secret to choose who sees it.

Spend XP

Only in your own character channel. Staff grant the XP.

What you have.

A menu of everything you can buy.

Memorise a spell you already know.

The price list.
Ranking up: Your new technique appears by itself. Shugenja pick three new spells, and /sheet learn reopens the picker.

Fight
