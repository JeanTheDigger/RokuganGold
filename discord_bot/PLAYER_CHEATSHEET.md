# Rokugan Bot: Player Cheat Sheet
*For everyone playing.*

Type `/` in any channel, pick a command, then fill in its options. `/help` opens the full manual.

*A | B means pick one. Words after a command are its options. You have one character, so you never need to name it.*

---

## Start here
- `/sheet create`  Build your character in a private channel. Staff approve it.
- `/whoami`  Your home screen, with buttons for Void, kata, gear and more.
- `/sheet view`  Your full character sheet.
- `/inventory`  Wield weapons, wear armor, drop things, check your purse.
- `/portrait set`  Add a picture of your character.
- `/help`  The full manual, by topic.

---

## Roll dice
- `/roll rolled kept`  Roll and Keep. Only you see it unless you change secret.
- `/dice 5k3`  The quick way. Also 7k2+5.
- `/check skill | social | stealth | investigate | lore | craft`  A skill roll against a TN your GM names. Every one but skill picks the trait for you.
- `/check drawback`  Roll the save a Disadvantage calls for, like Brash or Can't Lie.
- `/macro save | roll`  Save a roll you repeat, then roll it by name.
- `/history`  The latest rolls in this channel.
> Add `spend_void` to a check for +1k1. Use `secret` to choose who sees it.
> Your Advantages and Disadvantages apply to checks on their own. On a social check, name the `target`, like Crane or shugenja, when an Advantage depends on who you face.

---

## Void and abilities
- `/void status`  How many Void Points you have.
- `/void spend`  Spend one and say why.
- `/void refresh`  A Meditation roll to win one back.
- `/sheet kata activate`  Set your active kata.
- `/sheet kiho activate`  Turn a kiho on or off.
- `/sheet tattoo activate`  Set your active tattoo.

---

## Spend XP
> Only in your own character channel. Staff grant the XP.
- `/xp balance`  What you have.
- `/xp spend`  A menu of everything you can buy.
- `/xp trait | skill | emphasis | kata | kiho | advantage`  Buy one directly.
- `/xp spell`  Memorise a spell you already know.
- `/xp costs`  The price list.
> Ranking up: Your new technique appears by itself. Shugenja pick three new spells, and `/sheet learn` reopens the picker.

---

## Money and items
- `/pay member koku bu zeni`  Give money to another player.
- `/trade member item`  Give an item to another player.
- `/inventory`  See and manage what you carry.
> Staff hand out items found in play. Arrows run out as you shoot them.

---

## Fight
- `/combat setup`  Invite the players, then begin.
- `/combat start | join`  Or start a fight and join it yourself.
- `/combat board`  Post the board of action buttons.
- `/fight stance`  Pick this first. It locks once you act.
- `/fight attack`  Attack, with raises and maneuvers.
- `/fight custom_roll`  Ask staff to approve a roll no command covers.
- `/fight action | full_defense | guard`  Track your actions, or defend.
- `/combat turn done`  End your turn.
- `/combat status | recap`  The turn order, or the tally so far.
- `/combat end`  Close a fight that has only players in it.
> Your hits go to staff to approve. Everyone sees your wound level, never the count.

---

## Grapples, duels and Void
- `/grapple`  Start a grapple. Pick the two fighters from a menu.
- `/duel`  Start an Iaijutsu duel.
- `/combat void armor | initiative | swap`  Spend a Void Point in a fight.
- `/fight condition`  Ask staff to apply Dazed, Prone and the like.

---

## Magic
- `/spell cast`  Cast a spell you know.
- `/spell importune`  Ask the kami for a spell you do not know.
- `/spell list | search | view`  Browse every spell.
> You cast up to your School Rank in Mastery, one more in your Affinity element and one less in your Deficiency. New spells come only from rank-ups and from staff.

---

## Roleplay
- `/room create`  Make a private room and host it.
- `/room invite | kick | close`  Run these inside the room.
- `/letter send`  Write an in-character letter.
- `/letter list | read`  Your old letters.
- `/rumor list`  Rumors that reached you.
- `/location list`  The places on the server.
- `/date`  The in-game date.
- `/weather now`  Today's weather.

---

## Look things up
- `/ref search`  Search every catalogue at once.
- `/ref weapon | armor | advantage | kata | kiho | school | tattoo`  Pick one, then list, view or search.
- `/ref modifiers | calledshot | travel`  Quick combat and travel rules.
- `/ref encumbrance`  How much you can carry.
- `/compare`  Two characters side by side.
- `/players`  Who is on the server.
