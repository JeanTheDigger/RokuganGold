# s60. PC Integration — LOCKED

This section defines how player characters (PCs) exist within the persistent simulation. It covers presence, AP banking, action submission, offline behavior, and reactive auto-resolve policies. Networking and UI implementation are out of scope here.

---

## 60.1 PC Identity

A character is flagged as a PC by `is_pc: bool = true` on `L5RCharacterData`. Every PC is bound to exactly one player account via `player_id: String`. A player account may control at most one character at a time in a given world instance.

**Fields added to L5RCharacterData:**

| Field | Type | Default | Description |
|-------|------|---------|-------------|
| `is_pc` | `bool` | `false` | True if a human player controls this character |
| `player_id` | `String` | `""` | Account identifier of the owning player |
| `is_logged_in` | `bool` | `false` | True while the player has an active session |
| `home_settlement_id` | `int` | `-1` | Anchor for offline presence (letters arrive here) |
| `banked_ap` | `int` | `0` | AP accumulated while logged out, capped per s60.5 |
| `offline_policies` | `Dictionary` | `{}` | Auto-resolve rules for reactive events while offline |
| `bubble_scene_id` | `int` | `-1` | ID of active Bubble Time scene (-1 if none) |
| `bubble_anchor_ic_day` | `int` | `-1` | IC day at which current Bubble scene began |

## 60.2 Character Creation

Players create fresh characters at session start. The NPC decision engine never runs for a PC. A freshly created PC starts with:

- Clan, family, and school chosen by the player. **PCs may not take a monk school** (owner-locked 2026-06-06): monastic/kiho play is NPC-only. Enforced by `PcSystem.is_school_type_allowed_for_pc()` (rejects `SchoolType.MONK`) and validated by `PcSystem.is_valid_pc()`. Because kiho is monk-only (s38a A0), PCs consequently never learn kiho.
- Starting stats generated by standard world-generation rules (s52 Part 1), with the player allocating their trait and skill points manually via character creation UI.
- `physical_location` set to the starting settlement determined by their chosen school (family seat by default).
- `home_settlement_id` = starting `physical_location` (upgradeable later via lord-level play).
- Standing objectives assigned by their role (same rules as world-generated characters of equivalent rank).
- No history, no prior relationships, no prior CrimeRecords.

---

## 60.3 Logged-In Presence

When `is_logged_in == true`:

- The PC exists at their `physical_location` exactly like any NPC.
- Co-located NPCs observe them, interact with them, and may target them with social or covert actions.
- The NPC decision engine **does not run** for the PC. All actions are player-submitted.
- The PC is eligible to enter Bubble Time scenes (s60.7).

---

## 60.4 Logged-Out Presence (Home Anchor)

When `is_logged_in == false`:

- The PC disappears from the world. `physical_location` is set to `""`.
- No NPC can observe, target, or interact with the PC directly.
- Letters addressed to the PC arrive at `home_settlement_id`. They are delivered to the PC's `pending_letters` queue and presented on next login.
- Reactive events (duel challenges, favor requests, court invitations, training offers) append to the PC's `pending_events` queue and are processed by offline policies (s60.6) at the time of arrival.
- The simulation tick runs normally. The PC's AP accumulates in `banked_ap` each tick (s60.5).
- Physical effects that do not require the PC's active presence continue: stipend payments arrive, construction projects the PC ordered advance, their province resources tick normally.

**Home anchor:** `home_settlement_id` is the settlement where the PC is considered "in residence" while offline. It defaults to the PC's starting settlement and can be changed by a PC who holds a lord position (their primary seat becomes home). Otherwise it stays as last manually set or default.

**On login:** The PC reappears at `home_settlement_id`. Their `physical_location` is set to `home_settlement_id`. They receive a summary of events that occurred while offline (delivered letters, reactive event resolutions, world events at their location).

## 60.5 AP Banking

The simulation fires 4 ticks per real-world day (every 6 hours). A PC accumulates AP at each tick exactly as NPCs do, but instead of spending it in the NPC wave, it accrues to `banked_ap`.

**Accrual:** At each tick, the PC's daily AP allocation (determined by role and rank, same as NPCs) is added to `banked_ap`.

**Cap:** `banked_ap` is capped at **4× the PC's daily AP allocation** (representing one real-world day of accumulation). AP above the cap is lost. A player who logs in once per real day will have their full day's worth available.

**Spending:** When the player is logged in, they may spend `banked_ap` freely. Actions are queued against the player's `banked_ap` balance, not against a single tick's AP window.

**Tick behavior for logged-in PCs:** The NPC wave resolver skips PCs entirely (they have `is_pc = true`). AP for PCs is accrued to `banked_ap` instead of being set fresh each tick. A PC logged in mid-tick continues to hold their current `banked_ap` until they choose to act.

**Bubble Time hold:** AP is not spent while a PC is in an active Bubble scene (s60.7). Accrual continues. The queued actions from the scene resolve at scene end.

## 60.6 Offline Reactive Auto-Resolve Policies

While logged out, incoming reactive events are evaluated against the PC's `offline_policies` Dictionary. Policies are player-configured. Each policy entry maps a reactive event type to a resolution strategy.

**Supported event types:**

| Reactive Event Type | Default Policy | Notes |
|--------------------|----------------|-------|
| `DUEL_CHALLENGE_RECEIVED` | `QUEUE` | Holds until login |
| `FAVOR_REQUESTED` | `HONOR` | Auto-honors all offline favor requests |
| `COURT_INVITATION` | `QUEUE` | Holds until login |
| `ACCEPT_TRAINING` | `DECLINE` | Does not commit AP while offline |
| `CONTRACT_OFFERED` | `QUEUE` | Holds until login |

**Policy values:**

- `QUEUE` — Event is held in `pending_events` and presented to the player on login. No auto-resolution.
- `HONOR` / `ACCEPT` — Event is resolved affirmatively. Mechanical consequences apply immediately.
- `DECLINE` — Event is resolved negatively. Consequences apply immediately.
- `CONDITIONAL:<condition>` — Resolves affirmatively if the condition is true. Example: `CONDITIONAL:same_clan` accepts only same-clan invitations.

**Allowed conditions for CONDITIONAL:**

| Condition Key | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|
| `same_clan` | The initiating character shares the PC's clan |
| `disposition_friend` | PC's disposition toward initiator is ≥ 31 (Friend tier) |
| `higher_status` | Initiator's status is higher than PC's |
| `lower_status` | Initiator's status is lower than PC's |

Conditions can be combined with `AND` (space-separated): e.g. `CONDITIONAL:same_clan disposition_friend`.

**Queue cap:** `pending_events` for a PC may hold at most 30 queued events. Events beyond this cap are discarded silently (the initiating NPC receives no response — equivalent to being ignored).

## 60.7 Bubble Time Integration

Bubble Time scenes (s13) allow two or more characters to engage in open-ended RP without mechanical clock pressure.

**Starting a scene:** A PC may initiate a Bubble scene with any co-located character (PC or NPC). `bubble_scene_id` is set to a world-assigned integer. `bubble_anchor_ic_day` is set to the current IC day.

**During a scene:** The PC is considered occupied. They do not spend `banked_ap` outside the scene. They may declare scene actions that will resolve when the scene closes. No `physical_location` change is possible mid-scene.

**Closing a scene:** When all participants agree to close (or the last participant departs), outcomes are retroactively applied to `bubble_anchor_ic_day`. `bubble_scene_id` and `bubble_anchor_ic_day` reset to `-1`.

**AP cost of scene actions:** Actions declared within a Bubble scene spend from `banked_ap` at scene close. If `banked_ap` is insufficient at close time, lower-priority actions are dropped (player is informed).

**NPC participation:** An NPC pulled into a Bubble scene with a PC is considered occupied for the scene's IC duration. Their scheduled AP wave actions for `bubble_anchor_ic_day` are voided and replaced by scene outcomes (if any).

## 60.8 PC Interaction with NPC Systems

**Social targeting:** NPCs may target PCs normally. CHARM, GOSSIP, INTIMIDATE, COMMISSION_ASSASSINATION, and all other actions that can target NPCs may target PCs. Results enter the PC's display queue and are shown on next login (or in real time if logged in).

**Assassination operations against a PC:** Phase 1–3 tick normally. When the operation would produce a PC crisis event (`create_pc_crisis_event()`), it is presented to the player if logged in (real-time intervention window: method-specific grace period per s12.8). If the player is offline, the offline policy `ASSASSINATION_GRACE` (default: `QUEUE`) determines behavior — `QUEUE` freezes the operation until login, `ACCEPT_ENGINE_RESOLUTION` resolves via the NPC quality engine.

**Crime commission:** PCs can commit crimes. CrimeRecords are created normally. Investigation, prosecution, and sentencing follow the same rules as for NPCs.

**Death:** PC death is permanent (no resurrection, per hard constraint). The player account is dissociated from the character. The player may create a new character.

**Information scope:** The PC sees exactly what their character knows: `topic_pool`, `met_characters`, `knowledge_pool`, and provincial reports for their holdings. No out-of-character world knowledge is surfaced.

---

## 60.9 Simulation Exclusions for PCs

The following NPC-only systems do not apply to PCs:

- **NPC decision engine** — PCs never enter the AP wave resolver, the reactive event decision loop (they go to `offline_policies` instead), or the civilian order budget resolver.
- **Standing objective assignment** — The orchestrator does not auto-assign standing objectives to PCs. Players manage their own objectives via UI.
- **Strategic review** — PC lords do not run `_run_strategic_reviews()`. Their clan strategic priorities are set by the clan's NPC Champion if one exists.
- **Daily letter pass** — PCs do not auto-generate letters. Letter writing costs 1 AP and is player-initiated.

## 60.10 WorldState Fields

The following fields are added to `WorldStateData` to support PC integration:

| Field | Type | Default |
|-------|------|---------|
| `active_bubble_scenes` | `Array[Dictionary]` | `[]` |
| `next_bubble_scene_id` | `Array[int]` | `[1]` |

Each bubble scene dict: `{scene_id: int, participant_ids: Array[int], anchor_ic_day: int, queued_actions: Array[Dictionary]}`.
